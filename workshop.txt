description=On a public server running the mod, strangers can slip into your game as hostile intruders whose goal is to make your survival harder. Join a listed server through the new main menu entry to invade as the intruder.
description=
description=Features:
description=- Server browser filtered to servers running the mod, plus direct connect by IP
description=- Server-side loadout given to intruders on spawn (configurable)
description=- Intruders spawn near a random survivor and see everyone on the map
description=- Anonymous arrival alert (banner + alarm) for survivors
description=- Optional forced PvP when an intruder arrives
description=- Host controls (admin): open/close the invasion, kick, ban
description=- Max intruders limit and respawn cooldown on death
description=- Available in 12 languages
description=
description=Requires the server PVP option enabled for intruders to deal damage.
